The premise

Most plans assume a life you don't have: two free hours a day and a fridge full of prepped meals. This one starts from your calendar and builds the training and the eating around it.

The method

What you get, every week.

The same method in person and online. In person, most of it happens at your sessions. Online, it runs through your program, your weekly check-in and messages.

The fit

Who this is for.

A good fit

  • You work full time and your calendar is rarely your own.
  • You are roughly 30 to 50 and want to be leaner and stronger than you are now.
  • You have tried programs that assumed two free hours a day.
  • You want someone checking your work every week.

Not a fit

  • You want a crash diet or a 30-day transformation.
  • You need treatment for an injury or a medical condition. See your doctor or physical therapist first.
  • You want in-person sessions outside Miami-Dade. Online coaching is the option there.

Questions

Before you book.

What happens in the free intro session?

Thirty minutes with Lance, by video or in person in Miami-Dade. You go over your schedule, your training history and your goals, and you leave with a starting plan. There is no charge and no obligation to continue.

Where do in-person sessions happen?

At your home or in your building's gym, anywhere in Miami-Dade. Tell Lance what equipment you have and the sessions are planned around it. Some buildings ask outside trainers to register first, so check with your front desk.

Do I need a gym membership?

No. In-person sessions use your home setup or your building's gym. Online, a gym gives you the most options, but a home setup with dumbbells or a barbell works, and so does a hotel gym when you travel. Your program is written for the equipment you have.

How much time does it take?

Most plans run three to four hours a week, including walks and the weekly check-in. If you have less time, the plan is built for less.

I travel for work. Will this still work?

Yes. Tell your coach your travel days and you get hotel-gym or no-equipment sessions, plus a plan for eating on the road.

Will I get a meal plan?

You get calorie and protein targets and a simple structure built from foods you already eat. That is easier to keep up than a fixed menu.

I have not lifted in years. Is that a problem?

No. The first weeks start light and build from where you are.

Is this medical advice?

No. Marxen Fitness provides general fitness and nutrition coaching. It does not diagnose or treat medical conditions, and it is not a substitute for care from a doctor or a licensed dietitian. If you have a medical condition, check with your doctor before you start.
